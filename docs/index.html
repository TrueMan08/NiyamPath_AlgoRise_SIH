1. Executive Summary

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), through its Regulation Bureau, governs the standards, approvals, and quality assurance of technical education institutions throughout India. The statutory approval lifecycle encompasses initial application processing, document verification, multi-tier scrutiny, Expert Visit Committee (EVC) scheduling, post-approval conditional undertakings, and ongoing compliance monitoring.

Currently, the coordination of these distributed workflows faces significant operational friction due to disconnected tracking tools, manual document examination, non-standardized communication channels for deficiencies, and limited proactive alerting for upcoming statutory deadlines.

NiyamPath is proposed by Team AlgoRise as an integrated, unified regulatory workflow automation and compliance monitoring platform. Built upon an authoritative, role-based Core Workflow Engine and augmented by an assistive AI Intelligence Layer, NiyamPath centralizes the lifecycle of institutional approvals, streamlines deficiency resolution cycles, tracks inspection schedules, monitors post-approval undertakings, and manages document validity.

Core Operating Principle
NiyamPath adheres to strict Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) governance. The AI Intelligence Layer acts strictly as an advisory accelerator for document classification, metadata extraction, preliminary checklist comparison, and grounded knowledge retrieval. Statutory approval decisions, deficiency issuance, and authoritative stage transitions remain strictly with authorized AICTE officials.

4. Functional Modules Architecture

NiyamPath organizes institutional governance into ten dedicated functional modules operating on a unified relational data model:

  1. Application Lifecycle Management: Centralized repository tracking applications from initial draft and submission through scrutiny, resubmissions, inspections, and final determinations.
  2. Configurable Workflow Engine: Supports flexible stage definitions and transition validation rules (e.g., Scrutiny Only, Scrutiny + EVC, EVC + SAC/SHC) without hardcoding routes.
  3. Scrutiny & Deficiency Management: Formalizes officer observation recording, links deficiencies to specific requirements or documents, alerts institutions, and tracks resubmission cycles.
  4. Expert Visit Committee (EVC) Coordination: Manages committee assignment, inspection scheduling, visit tracking, and post-inspection report submission.
  5. Post-Approval Compliance Monitoring: Tracks conditional approvals and institutional undertakings with explicit due dates, evidence submissions, and officer verification workflows.
  6. Centralized Document & Validity Registry: Maintains document metadata, issue dates, expiry dates, version history, and automated renewal queues for expiring leases and safety certificates.
  7. Notifications & SLA Escalations: Event-driven notification dispatching alerts for deficiency issuance, stage advancements, compliance deadlines, and automated hierarchical escalations.
  8. Executive Reports & Analytics: Provides administrative dashboards depicting stage-wise pendency distribution, regional workload, and application ageing calculated from platform data.
  9. Immutable Audit Trail: Records tamper-evident, actor-attributed logs of every state mutation, deficiency issuance, document renewal, and officer review of AI suggestions.
  10. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Backend-enforced authorization across five personas: Institution User, Central Scrutiny Officer, Regional Officer, EVC Member, and Administrator.

5. AI Architecture and Assistive Capabilities

The AI Intelligence Layer provides purpose-driven automation designed to alleviate administrative overhead while maintaining absolute regulatory integrity:

5.1 Workstream C: AI Document Intelligence

  • Document Parsing & Local OCR: Extracts digital vector text via PyMuPDF (fitz), with Tesseract OCR fallback for scanned certificates.
  • Classification & Field Extraction: Automatically categorizes uploaded files (e.g. Registered Lease Deed, Land Title, Structural Stability Certificate, Fire NOC) and extracts key-value pairs (institution name, registered dates, expiry dates, land area in acres, issuing authority).
  • Checklist Validation & Omission Detection: Compares extracted data against mandatory AICTE guidelines to identify missing mandatory fields or expired certificates before formal officer scrutiny.
  • Cross-Document Discrepancy Detection: Detects conflicting information across submitted documents (e.g. discrepancy between campus address on lease deed versus application form).
  • Draft Scrutiny Generation: Synthesizes provisional deficiency notices citing specific document pages, enabling officers to review, edit, or reject observations in a single click.

5.2 Workstream D: AI Knowledge Intelligence & RAG

  • Regulatory Document Ingestion & Chunking: Parses official AICTE regulatory circulars and Approval Process Handbook excerpts into hierarchical semantic chunks preserving Chapter, Section, and Appendix metadata.
  • Dense Vector Search: Uses dense embeddings and vector storage (ChromaDB) to retrieve relevant regulatory passages.
  • Grounded Regulatory Assistant: Synthesizes source-cited answers to user queries. If a query falls outside the ingested knowledge base, the assistant strictly returns: "The approved AICTE knowledge base does not contain this information."
  • Pendency & Bottleneck Insights: Analyzes historical stage durations to identify workflow bottlenecks (e.g., regional EVC committee availability) and flag at-risk applications.
AI Governance Guardrail
AI outputs are strictly classified as provisional suggestions. The AI service has no direct database access and possesses zero authority to approve or reject applications, issue statutory deficiencies, or advance workflow stages.

8. End-to-End Workflow Scenario

To illustrate the end-to-end operation of NiyamPath, we trace the journey of an institutional approval application:

  1. Submission: Aryavart Institute of Technology creates an application for Extension of Approval (EOA), uploads required deeds, certificates, and affidavits, and submits it to the portal.
  2. Initial Ingestion & Document Analysis: The AI Document Intelligence engine extracts registration numbers, issue dates, and expiry dates, flagging that the institution's structural safety certificate expired 60 days prior.
  3. Scrutiny: A Central Scrutiny Officer opens the scrutiny workspace. The system displays the extracted metadata alongside the flagged certificate and presents a suggested draft deficiency.
  4. Deficiency Issuance: The officer reviews the draft, adjusts the compliance deadline to 15 days, and issues the official deficiency. The application state updates to DEFICIENCY_RAISED.
  5. Resubmission: The institution receives an instant notification, obtains a renewed structural fitness certificate, and uploads the replacement document.
  6. Resolution & Forwarding: The officer re-reviews the renewed certificate, verifies compliance, marks the deficiency resolved, and forwards the application to the EVC stage.
  7. EVC Scheduling: The Regional Officer selects an expert committee and schedules a physical inspection for November 20. EVC members receive the inspection packet.
  8. Inspection & Conditional Approval: Following the visit, the committee submits an electronic inspection report. The Council grants approval subject to a 60-day undertaking for additional laboratory equipment.
  9. Compliance Monitoring & Verification: The system schedules an automated undertaking reminder at 30 days and 7 days. The institution uploads procurement invoices. An authorized officer verifies the evidence and marks the undertaking COMPLETED.

9. Security, Role-Based Access Control, and Governance

  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Handled at the Spring Boot controller and service layer. Institution users cannot view regional dashboards or inspect peer applications.
  • Data Minimization: The AI service receives only purpose-specific context required for extraction or verification; no arbitrary student or institutional financial data is exposed.
  • Immutable Audit Logging: Every action emits an AuditEvent capturing actor ID, role, action, entity ID, previous state, new state, and timestamp, stored in a tamper-evident audit table.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Governance: Statutory decisions and legal deficiencies require explicit human confirmation. AI is structurally prohibited from mutating authoritative state.

11. Limitations, Governance Boundaries, and Assumptions

Explicit Prototype Boundaries

In accordance with rigorous academic and engineering standards, we explicitly declare the boundaries of this project:

  • No Direct Production Access: NiyamPath has not been connected to live internal AICTE production databases or private APIs.
  • Demonstration Data: The frontend prototype operates on realistic, fictional demonstration datasets. Dashboard figures and institutional records represent sample scenarios.
  • Workflow Rule Verification: Specific workflow routes and stage sequences (e.g. SAC/SHC) remain configurable and must be verified against authoritative AICTE Approval Process Handbooks prior to production deployment.
  • AI Output Verification: Extracted fields, discrepancy indicators, and scrutiny suggestions are advisory and must not be treated as autonomous statutory decisions.
